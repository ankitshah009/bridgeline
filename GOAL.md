Product: mobile-web front door that gets residents of SF affordable housing into tech training. Last-dollar grant paid to providers and vendors, never cash to residents. Free programs first (Per Scholas, TechSF/JVS, Free City College, SF Public Library), then public aid (WIOA ITA up to $6,000 for tuition and materials, state-listed providers only; Workforce Pell later), then the grant covers what's left: child care, transit, device, exam fees, and tuition only when no free option fits.

Tiers: Starter $500 (device, exam, short course; goal + navigator check-in). Stay-in-Training $2,500 (child care, transit, equipment, cert fees during a free program; admission). Career $6,000 (tuition + support; admission, funding check, career plan). Tuition paid 50% enrollment / 30% completion / 20% verified job or raise. Decision within 10 business days. No ISAs or required loans. Race and immigration status never collected.

Benefits shield (HUD 24 CFR 5.609): education grants for tuition, books and fees are excluded; supportive reimbursements are excluded only for publicly assisted programs (private funding: confirm with counsel); cash stipends may count as income. So: pay vendors directly, no stipends, run a benefits check before every award, and flag LIHTC, public housing, PBV and HCV differences. This is planning guidance, not legal advice.
